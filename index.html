Who we are

One partner. Integrated delivery.

Madunge Events and Projects is a proudly South African, 100% Black-owned events, infrastructure and project management company headquartered in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.

We work with corporate organisations, government institutions, brands, event professionals and private clients to turn plans into professionally delivered experiences. Our integrated approach brings planning, event infrastructure, logistics, hospitality, activations and project coordination together through one accountable partner.
