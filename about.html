01

Our story

Proudly South African. Built to deliver.

Madunge Events and Projects is a 100% Black-owned events, infrastructure and project management company headquartered in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, with the capability to deliver projects across South Africa.

We work with corporate organisations, government institutions, brands, event professionals, venues and private clients to turn plans into professionally delivered experiences.

Our integrated approach brings planning, event infrastructure, logistics, hospitality, activations and project coordination together through one accountable partner. Every solution is tailored to the client's objectives, audience, budget, venue and timeline.

Leadership & experience

Commercial insight. Practical delivery.

Established in 2005, Madunge Events and Projects has built experience delivering events, infrastructure and projects for a range of clients and industries.

Madunge Events and Projects is led by Managing Executive Sifiso Kalinjani, a marketing and business professional with experience in corporate management, marketing strategy, events, promotions and stakeholder engagement.

His professional background includes experience associated with The Holiday Club, African Bank, FNB, Absa and MTN South Africa, where he served as a Marketing Manager. He also previously chaired the MTN KwaZulu-Natal Regional Executive Committee.

His governance experience includes non-executive directorships and chairperson roles associated with music, tourism and film industry organisations.
